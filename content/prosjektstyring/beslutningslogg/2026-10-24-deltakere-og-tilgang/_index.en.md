## Decision

For the time being, the following applies to who can be part of the GitHub organisation samt-x:

- **Anyone can propose new participants.**
- **The core team approves** new participants and removes those who are no longer part of the work. Anyone in the core team can do this.
- **The owners of the organisation are referred to by role**, not by name, on the public site. Members can see the names in GitHub.

The arrangement is **provisional**.

## Background

Until now, access has been granted as needed, without anything written down about who can propose or approve new participants. The core team now has a member list that is checked against GitHub, and that calls for a clear arrangement for who decides.

## Further work

A shared overview of roles and tasks in the governance and administration of the ecosystem is to be put in place. For now we assume that SAMT-BU is one such ecosystem, but the term needs a closer definition. The work is followed up in [samt-bu-architecture#34](https://github.com/samt-x/samt-bu-architecture/issues/34). Once the overview exists, it replaces this arrangement.
