AI+ Offentlig sektor (AI+ Public sector) is one of Norway's most important meeting places for responsible and practical use of artificial intelligence in the public sector. Leaders, professionals, researchers and technologists meet here to share experiences of developing and adopting sound AI services that strengthen our public missions. A ticket to AI+ Offentlig sektor costs NOK 990 (plus VAT).
