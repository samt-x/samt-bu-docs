Data Week 2026 is a two-day in-person conference held in Oslo on 5–6 May. The event is organised by the Big Data Value Association (BDVA) together with the BDVA members Institute for Energy Technology (IFE), the Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU), SINTEF and Western Norway Research Institute (Vestlandsforsking).

The conference offers a leading platform for regional, European and international actors to showcase results, share expertise and engage with the wider data and AI community.

**Theme: "Data Fjords: Unlocking AI for Industry and Society"**

The theme of Data Week 2026 is "Data Fjords: Unlocking AI for Industry and Society", focusing on the fundamental elements of creating value from data. Artificial intelligence (AI) is a key tool for unlocking the value of data in various ways. Creating value from data also requires an ecosystem perspective and collaboration between multiple actors. This forms the basis for excellent research, which must quickly be put into action to strengthen competitiveness and deliver societal benefit.

The programme is updated continuously and is available [here](https://data-week.eu/2026-edition/programme/).
